What the research of 2026-11-21, the first of twenty-two cards on two
engines, showed the research command to lack. The run is
`research/2026-11-21-describing-ux-ui-in-text/`; every point below
stands in its Deviations. First, the principal's own: a deep research
hands out one assignment for the deep research built into other
tools and keeps what comes back in its own bundle, so that every
output of a research lies in one place. He had the assignment
written from the confirmed plan, ran it by hand in two outside tools
and had the assignment and both reports placed in the bundle; the
skill has no step for any of it, and it was done against three rules
(a registered source moved, an immutable bundle added to, one report
not in English). To be settled there: whether `/research --deep`
writes the assignment as a step of its own, and when; where a
returned report is registered and under which name; whether the
reports enter the synthesis, get a comparison of their own or stay
beside it; what immutable means for a bundle that receives reports
after its synthesis; what the document kinds and the template of the
plan then say a bundle holds. Beside it, four observations that are
Claude's: the skill names a note `<card>.<engine>.md` while
`scripts/forge-cite.py --independence` reads `card-*.md` only; the
return of an agent on the session model reaches the session as a
message alone, so that the skill's "Claude writes it down" meant
typing a note over by hand, and from card 2 on the agents wrote
their own notes; the session's web search ran out when twenty-one
agents ran in three waves, and the skill says neither how many run
at once nor what an agent does when a search is refused; and under
the profile the script gives it the second engine read the web
through its search tool's summaries only, which is why fourteen of
its twenty-two notes cite addresses that do not open and why the
second run the skill prescribes did not help. Closed when the intent
says what is decided on each of the five and the skill, the
scripts, the template and CLAUDE.md follow. Neighbour: THR.0740, on
sending work to another engine without the principal carrying it.
Claude's view, his own: the assignment belongs right after the
plan's confirmation, and the reports beside the notes and out of the
synthesis, since they cannot be checked as the notes are. Opened
2026-11-21.
