The names of the parts: what the forge is made of and what each
part is called. The principal plans the forge as three parts and
later a family: a general base under every forge (histories, the
ledger, research, the mechanics of critique, challenge and check),
an operating layer per forge (elicitation, the concrete lenses,
personas and checks) and the project in which an operating layer
is designed by its own process. Four steps: to 7.0 one monolith;
at 7.0 two blocks, the base with the operating layer, and the
definition; next three blocks, the base turned into a plugin of
Claude Code; last one base under three forges, of thought, of
tests and of UX/UI with the web prototype, their definitions in
separate projects. Working names taken 2026-11-09, temporary:
Anvil for the base, Forge of Proof for tests, Forge of Form for
UX/UI and the prototype. Proposed by Claude, not decided:
`framework` for an operating layer, `definition` for its project,
the word `engine` no longer for the base or the repository and
kept where it means a model or a converter, the rewrite of the
existing files left to the split into three blocks. What prompted
it: `engine` today means the whole repository, a model of another
family (`--engine`, `forge-engine.py`) and a converter (`md2pptx
--engine`), `core` stands beside it and the operating layer has no
name. Open: the vocabulary as a whole, not to be hurried into a
decision; the picture of the four steps: the recipe
`recipes/forge-evolution.md` 0.1 and its render exist since
2026-11-09 and the principal judged the render not good, to be
finished; what is wrong with it he has not yet said, and how the
mermaid grid draws is unverified. The renderer reported where the
legend and this thread differ: the names shown as settled though
they are working names, Anvil as the base of every AI project (the
principal's words) against "under every forge" here, a solid
outline called a repository; and the recipe's "equal boxes" against
the Anvil of step 4 spanning three columns. Opened 2026-11-09.
