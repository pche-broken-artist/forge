One head against several: the forge is built for a single user,
whose own head holds the answers, and collides with work in which
the answers lie with several people. Shown by discussion #19 of
2026-11-25, by Entir-42, which proposes a working method "Overview"
beside the walkthrough (POS.0850): the principal sees every open
item at once, groups the items by the person who can answer them and
selects what is worked now and in what order, every verdict still
given in a walkthrough; its premise is that many items cannot be
decided by the principal alone, the answer lying with a stakeholder
offline. The principal's word of 2026-11-26: the matter is larger
than the proposal, a collision of single user and multi-user, and
nearly a conceptual decision; set aside for deeper thought and not
worked on now, the author told so in the discussion. Read in the
engine that day: the ledger's Waiting on principal already gives
every open matter as one line (POS.1620); a walkthrough works its
list in order of weight and nothing says that the principal selects
the items or their order (`.claude/skills/walkthrough/SKILL.md`); an
owner is carried by a TBC alone; an answer found outside has its way
in, `/ingest` and then the intent by the principal's act, and a
question has no way out. Beside it: multi-principal use (THR.0090),
several principals each in his own instance. To be settled: whether
the forge stays the instrument of one head or takes the work of
several heads into its conversation, and what of the Overview
follows from that. Closed when that decision stands in the intent,
or the matter is dropped, and the discussion is answered. Claude's
view, his own and not verified: the premise reaches the base, since
elicitation draws the answer from the principal's head (prime
directive 1) and, where it is not there, the walkthrough leaves him
`park` alone, a verdict with a record for an item nobody decided; a
list of questions for one person may be an output for an audience
rather than the tracking of people; and who leads the conversation
is a question of a single principal with a long list as well. Opened
2026-11-26.
