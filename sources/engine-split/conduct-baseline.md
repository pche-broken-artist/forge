## The kinds of case since the change

1. A proposal misses what was asked. 2026-11-10: a poster built the
   other way round from what the brief of its project says.
   2026-11-15: a renaming of the vocabulary proposed as a rewrite of
   every text. 2026-11-16: the question who owns the reviewers'
   dispatchers put forward when the cut of CLAUDE.md was wanted.
2. Derives wrongly from a document that already says it. 2026-11-10:
   the principal steered Claude step by step to a programme that
   already stood in the brief.
3. Touches what nobody asked for, and more widely than needed.
   2026-11-12 and 2026-11-13: the comment of the history template
   changed twice, 84 files touched. 2026-11-12: the closing of a
   thread that the principal had to stop.
4. Turns round when opposed. 2026-11-11: after an objection Claude
   reversed his proposal whole instead of keeping what held and
   saying his own remarks.
5. Builds more than the matter needs. 2026-11-12: a questionnaire
   point by point at the birth of a thread. 2026-11-14: a render
   through a recipe proposed for a text whose input never changes.
6. Speaks abstractly or cannot be understood. 2026-11-14: a division
   of rules put as isolated decisions, which the principal said he
   did not understand. 2026-11-15: a rule in prose where he needed to
   see examples. 2026-11-10: a sentence on the poster he could not
   read as his own language.
7. Brings up old matters nobody spoke of. 2026-11-10: the splitting
   of large files raised from a thread and from the commit history.
8. Does not read to the end what it ran. 2026-11-13: only the
   beginning of a check's output over two projects was read.

Not found: the case the principal remembers, documents of another
project worked on while the session was in the forge's own project.

## How it was counted, and its limits

A strong sign is a message that carries one of a fixed list of words
and phrases of displeasure or of correction, or an interruption; a
verdict is a message that opens with one of the five letters. The
kinds were read by Claude from the forty messages the list picked on
Fable 5.2, each with what Claude had said and run before it, not from
the whole logs. The sieve is by words and rough; the work since the
change was harder than before it, the split, the vocabulary and the
map; eight sessions are a small sample, and the day of the
measurement is among them. A repeat counts with the same list.
