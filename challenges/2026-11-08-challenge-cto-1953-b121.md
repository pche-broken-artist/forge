# Peer review (cto) — 2026-11-08

## Overall read

Fourteen days after the major, the intent stands at 6.23 and 3 114 lines, and the fortnight was spent well by any engineering standard: a test suite with CI where there was none, the forge's own project cut out of the engine and rehearsed on a copy first, a layer of the chain built by a second person in his own clone and merged, the `shall` question answered as a rule of kind. Two of the four majors of the last review were rejected with reasons on record, two were parked "until 6.x", and 6.x has now run twenty-three minors without touching either. What I find this time is a displacement rather than a defect: the forge's largest open architectural question, the split into an engine and a framework, is being answered by a prototype in a side project whose brief the forge's own chain does not cite, while the chain's thread on it still records "nothing decided"; the release has quietly acquired a mode in which Claude settles findings and pushes without a reading; and the first real use of the chain by someone other than the principal, which the last review was told would be the evidence, arrived and was recorded as a merge. The forge is still getting better at being the forge; the thing it is for is now happening partly outside its own sight.
