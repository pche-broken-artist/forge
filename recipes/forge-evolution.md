# Recipe — the picture of the forge's parts in four steps

## Inputs
- threads/THR.0850.md

## Instructions
One picture with its legend, nothing else: no introduction, no
commentary. The names and the four steps are the thread's; where the
thread and these Instructions differ, the picture follows these
Instructions and the render says where they differ, under the link,
instead of choosing.

The picture, white ground, one sans-serif font, drawn at twice its
size and scaled down so that the edges are smooth:
- Title at the top left: "From a monolith to a family of forges".
- Four steps left to right, each headed on two lines: "Step 1" bold
  over "up to 7.0"; "Step 2" over "at 7.0"; "Step 3" over "next";
  "Step 4" over "goal", the second line smaller and grey, so that the
  number of a step never reads as a version.
- A block is a box of its own: a rounded frame with a gap between
  blocks. The layers of one block share its frame, divided by a thin
  line only.
- Every step has the same height and the steps start and end on one
  line.
- Step 1: one block, definition over framework over Anvil. Step 2: two
  blocks, definition above, framework over Anvil below. Step 3: three
  blocks, definition, framework, Anvil, one above another.
- Step 4 turns: Anvil one narrow block the whole height at the left;
  beside it a column of the frameworks, one per forge; at the right a
  column of the definitions, one per forge; one forge per row:
  Forge of Thought, Forge of Proof, Forge of Form.
- A box names its layer above, plain, and its forge below, bold, by
  the forge's whole name: "Forge of Thought", never "Thought". Anvil
  carries its name alone.
- One fill per layer, the same in every step: definition a light
  beige, framework a light blue, Anvil a light grey.
- The legend under the picture, a term in bold and its meaning: a
  swatch of its fill before definition, framework and Anvil, none
  before the three forges; then a grey line of its own. The terms and
  meanings:
  - definition — the project in which a framework is designed by its
    own process
  - framework — the operating layer of one forge: what belongs to its
    field, built on Anvil
  - Anvil — the general base under every forge: histories, the
    ledger, research, the mechanics of critique, challenge and check;
    from step 3 a plugin of Claude Code
  - Forge of Thought — the forging of thoughts: elicitation, the
    lenses, personas and checks
  - Forge of Proof — tests; content not yet set
  - Forge of Form — UX/UI and the web prototype; content not yet set
  - the grey line: "Anvil, Forge of Proof and Forge of Form are
    working names."
